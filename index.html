The place

A small counter, full of detail.

Cream stucco under a timber roof, the logo on the wall, a wavy mirror by the window. Along the front, in dark letters: A Softer Start to Your Day.

A courtyard to linger in.

Gravel underfoot, olive-green chairs, and round white tables whose perforated tops throw dappled shadows. At Ghurnata, in Heliopolis, the arches and tower of a heritage building stand behind the tables.
